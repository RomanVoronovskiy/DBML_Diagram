# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the DBML Diagram community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for users and the community.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and political or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
